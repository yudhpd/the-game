THE FLOOR

Multiplayer territory trivia showdown

How many players?

How to play

  • The floor is a grid with one square per player, each showing their chosen area of expertise.
  • On your turn, challenge any opponent's square that touches your territory — each category can only ever be challenged once.
  • Each duel gives both players a 45-second life clock. Only the active player's clock runs.
  • Say your guess out loud — the Game Master marks it Correct (switch turns) or Pass (-3s, same player continues).
  • Whoever's clock hits zero first loses — and is eliminated on the spot, along with all of their territory, which the winner inherits.
  • If a category runs out of fresh questions mid-duel, it ends immediately — whoever has more time left on their clock wins.
  • Win a duel and choose to keep playing, or pass — a randomizer picks someone who hasn't gone yet.
  • The final showdown between the last two players is a best-of-3: round 1 is the defender's category, round 2 is the challenger's, and a 1–1 tie is broken by a category the Game Master picks from ones nobody selected all game.
  • Last player standing controls the whole floor and wins.
